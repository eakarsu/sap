⏺ GenAI Scenarios for SAP Environments

  SAP provides strong transactional and operational backbone, but GenAI can fill gaps SAP doesn't cover natively. Here are practical scenarios grouped by domain:

  ---
  1. Finance & Controlling (FICO)

  - Invoice anomaly explanation — GenAI analyzes blocked invoices and generates plain-language explanations for why they were flagged, suggesting resolution steps.
  - Financial report narratives — Auto-generate management commentary from SAP financial statements (P&L, balance sheet).
  - Audit question answering — A chatbot that queries SAP data and answers auditor questions in natural language.

  2. Supply Chain & Procurement (MM/SD)

  - Purchase order drafting — Generate PO descriptions, terms, and supplier communications from requisition data.
  - Demand sensing summaries — Combine SAP forecast data with external signals (news, weather) and summarize supply risk in natural language.
  - Supplier evaluation reports — Auto-generate supplier scorecards by pulling SAP vendor master + GR/IR data and writing narrative assessments.

  3. Sales & Customer Experience (SD/CRM)

  - Customer inquiry auto-response — GenAI reads order status, delivery dates, and pricing from SAP and drafts customer-facing email responses.
  - Quote generation — From a customer conversation or email, extract requirements and auto-create a quotation in SAP.
  - Churn risk explanation — Analyze customer order patterns from SAP and generate a narrative explaining why a customer may be at risk.

  4. Human Resources (HCM/SuccessFactors)

  - Job description generation — Pull position details from SAP org structure and generate tailored job postings.
  - Employee onboarding guides — Auto-generate personalized onboarding checklists based on role, department, and location from SAP HR data.
  - Policy Q&A chatbot — Answer employee HR questions by grounding GenAI on company policies stored alongside SAP HR master data.

  5. Manufacturing & Plant Maintenance (PP/PM)

  - Maintenance work order summaries — Summarize equipment history, past notifications, and suggest root causes for recurring failures.
  - Quality inspection narratives — Generate human-readable quality reports from QM inspection lot data.
  - SOP generation — Draft standard operating procedures from existing routing and BOM data.

  7. Integration & Extensibility

  - BTP + GenAI side-by-side extensions — Deploy GenAI models on SAP BTP that consume SAP S/4HANA APIs to provide intelligent recommendations without modifying the
  SAP core.
  - RAG over SAP documentation — Build a Retrieval-Augmented Generation system over SAP Notes, OSS messages, and custom documentation to help basis/functional teams
  troubleshoot faster.
  - Email/ticket-to-SAP automation — GenAI parses unstructured emails or support tickets, extracts structured data, and creates SAP transactions (service orders,
  complaints, etc.).

  ---
  Key Takeaway

  SAP excels at structured transactions and processes. GenAI excels at unstructured data, natural language, and content generation. The highest-value scenarios sit at
   the boundary — where unstructured input needs to become structured SAP data, or where SAP data needs to be explained in human-friendly language.
